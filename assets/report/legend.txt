Fields:
Reference Text: Ground truth (real) text
Generated Text: Sampled text
Reference Audio: Ground truth (real) audio
Generated Audio: Sampled audio
Whisper Transcription: Transcription of the generated audio using a pre-trained Whisper speech-to-text model. Whisper-medium is used for lightweight evaluation during callbacks; Whisper-large is used for full-scale evaluation

Metrics:
Word Error Rate (WER): Word-level edit distance between the reference and generated text: {insertions + deletions + substitutions}/{number of words in the reference}
Character Error Rate (CER): Character-level edit distance between the reference and generated text: {insertions + deletions + substitutions}/{number of characters in the reference}
UTMOS: Neural predictor of human Mean Opinion Score (MOS) for generated speech, on a 1-5 scale. Evaluates how natural the generated audio sounds
Generative Perplexity (GenPPL): Perplexity of the generated text as computed by Llama-3.2-1B. Evaluates the coherence and fluency of the generated sentences
Speaker Similarity (SpkSim): Cosine similarity between the WavLM-base-plus-sv embeddings of the generated and reference audio. Measures how well the voice in the synthesized signal matches the voice in the reference audio
Frechet Speech Distance (FSD): Adaptation of the Frechet Inception Distance to speech, with features extracted from WavLM-large. Compares the distributions of real and generated audio
LLM-as-Judge-MOS: Evaluates the coherence of the generated text continuation with the true continuation, on a 1-5 scale, as judged by GPT-4o
SALMON: Assesses the model's ability to recognize paralinguistic properties. SALMON is originally likelihood-based, requiring models to compute the likelihood of a true and a synthetic continuation of an audio prompt. Since likelihood-based evaluation is not representative of diffusion models, a generative variant is used instead: the model produces its own continuation, which an external judge model compares to the true and synthetic continuations to determine which one it more closely resembles
- TITANET: sentiment, speaker, gender
- HuBERT-large-audioset: background (domain), background (random)
- Wav2Vec2-large-audioset: room

Task-Specific Metrics:
Joint Generation:
- Cross-Modal WER: WER between the generated text and the transcription of the generated audio
- Cross-Modal CER: CER between the generated text and the transcription of the generated audio
- GenPPL-text: GenPPL for the generated text
- GenPPL-speech: GenPPL for the transcription of the generated audio
Text-to-Speech:
- ASR-WER: WER between the reference text and the transcription of the generated audio
- ASR-CER: CER between the reference text and the transcription of the generated audio
Speech-to-Text:
- Clean-WER: WER on LibriSpeechPC.test.clean
- Clean-CER: CER on LibriSpeechPC.test.clean
- Other-WER: WER on LibriSpeechPC.test.other
- Other-CER: CER on LibriSpeechPC.test.other
Continuation:
- GenPPL-text: GenPPL for the generated text
- GenPPL-speech: GenPPL for the transcription of the generated audio
